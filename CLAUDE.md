- `conduct.interview-when-input-is-needed` (always): Interview the user when their input is needed. When a decision is genuinely the user's, and neither the request, the code, the architecture docs, the design system nor a sensible default settles it, interview the user: a focused round of questions with concrete options, repeated until the answer is clear. Do not guess the answer, and do not stop with a vague hand-back in place of the questions. A decision that can be inferred is made and stated, not asked.
